Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026  |  Application: XayLens (com.amanikbt1.xaylens)

Privacy Summary

XayLens is an offline-first camera application. We do not require user accounts, we do not collect personal data, and we never upload your photos, videos, audio, or facial geometry to any external server or cloud service. All face lenses and visual effects run 100% locally on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to XayLens ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how XayLens (Android package com.amanikbt1.xaylens and web application) handles information and device permissions when you use our creative camera and real-time face lens application.

2. Face Data & Biometric Information Disclosure

To apply interactive augmented reality (AR) effects and distortion lenses (such as Big Nose, Big Eyes, Tiny Face, Wide Face, Alien, Puppy, Bunny, and Funny Glasses), XayLens estimates 2D facial landmark regions (such as the relative positions of the eyes, nose, mouth, and forehead).

  • How Face Data Is Processed: All facial landmark positioning and mesh transformations are computed ephemerally in real-time memory (RAM) directly on your device while the camera is active.
  • No Biometric Identification: XayLens does not perform facial recognition, user authentication, or biometric identification.
  • No Storage or Retention: Facial coordinate data is discarded immediately after each video frame is rendered on your screen. It is never saved to disk, database, or persistent storage.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Facial landmark data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to or shared with any third parties, advertisers, or cloud AI services.

3. Device Permissions We Request

XayLens requests only the hardware permissions strictly necessary to operate as a camera application:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to display the live camera preview, render real-time face lenses on screen, capture photos, and record videos when you press the shutter button.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely to record audio alongside video when you actively record a video clip in Video Mode.
  • Photo Library / Media Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used only when you explicitly tap the "Save Media" button to save a captured photo or recorded video to your device's local gallery (under the XayLens album).

You may revoke any of these permissions at any time through your device's system Settings.

4. Local Preferences Storage

XayLens stores basic camera configuration preferences locally on your device (using local on-device storage) so your settings persist between sessions:

  • Preferred flash mode (Off, Auto, or On)
  • Default camera facing (Front or Back)
  • Mirror front camera toggle
  • Haptic feedback and video quality toggles

You can clear and reset all stored preferences to factory defaults at any time inside the in-app Settings → Reset Preferences to Default button, or by uninstalling the application.

5. Data Collection, Analytics & Third-Party Services

XayLens does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal information, usage telemetry, location coordinates, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or cloud tracking scripts.

6. Children's Privacy

Because XayLens operates completely offline on your device and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user of any age, the application complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global child data protection standards.
